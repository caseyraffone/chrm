# CHRM 14-Day Finance Reps Launch Sprint

## Launch offer

**Free Finance Interview Rep:** choose an IB, PE, markets, or fit question; answer by voice or typing; receive a score and a tighter version.

All links point to the finance browser experience with campaign tags.

## Content pillars

1. **Answer challenge** — give candidates a question and a time limit.
2. **Before and after** — show a weak answer, feedback, and a stronger redo.
3. **Recruiting truth** — correct vague or misleading interview advice.
4. **Build in public** — Casey explains why CHRM exists and what is being learned.
5. **Product proof** — short, honest demonstrations of the working rep loop.

## Ready-to-record scripts

### DCF challenge

**On-screen hook:** "IB interview test: explain a DCF in 30 seconds."

**Spoken script:**  
"Start with projected unlevered free cash flow. Discount it using WACC. Calculate terminal value, discount that too, and add everything for enterprise value. Then bridge to equity value. Think you can say it cleanly under pressure? Try the full question free on CHRM."

**Visual:** Casey on camera for the hook, then a CHRM screen recording showing the question and score.

### Why banking

**On-screen hook:** "If your Why Banking answer starts with 'I've always loved finance,' restart."

**Spoken script:**  
"A strong answer connects a real past experience to what bankers actually do: transactions, analysis, fast learning, and team execution. Generic interest is not enough. CHRM makes you answer out loud, then shows where the story loses specificity."

### Before and after

**On-screen hook:** "The first answer sounded informed. It still scored 5 out of 10."

**Structure:**

1. Play or display the weak opening.
2. Show the score and one missing key point.
3. Show the tighter structure.
4. Repeat the improved answer.
5. CTA to attempt the same question.

Do not present a fabricated person as a real user. Use Casey's own answer or label the demonstration "example answer."
